04 Face Liveness Detection and Presentation Attack Detection for Registration and Biometric Authentication Enable the Desktop and Android Registration Clients to process face image streams from biometric devices, perform face liveness and Presentation Attack Detection (PAD), and automatically guide residents, operators, and supervisors through active facial challenges when required to establish that the captured face belongs to a live person. Complexity Level: High Overview The Registration Client shall support face liveness detection and Presentation Attack Detection (PAD) for face-based biometric workﬂows using face image streams received from supported L0/L1 biometric devices . The feature shall be applicable to: ● Resident face capture during registration ● Operator biometric authentication ● Supervisor biometric authentication The Registration Client shall receive the face frame stream from the connected biometric device and use the conﬁgured liveness/PAD capability to determine whether the face presented to the device belongs to a live person and whether a presentation attack is being attempted. The liveness veriﬁcation shall follow a hybrid passive and active approach . Initially, the system shall perform passive liveness detection using the incoming face frames without requiring the user to perform any speciﬁc action. The liveness engine shall generate a quality/conﬁdence score based on the observed facial characteristics and available liveness indicators. The Registration Client shall compare the passive liveness result against a conﬁgurable threshold. ● If the liveness quality meets the conﬁgured threshold and no presentation attack is detected, the face capture or authentication workﬂow shall proceed. ● If the liveness quality does not meet the conﬁgured threshold, the system shall automatically initiate active liveness veriﬁcation .

### PAGE 2 ---

● During active veriﬁcation, the Registration Client shall prompt the user to perform dynamically selected facial actions. ● The system shall analyse the subsequent frame stream to determine whether the requested action was performed by a live person. ● The face capture or authentication workﬂow shall proceed only when the conﬁgured liveness and PAD criteria are satisﬁed. The user shall not select the challenge. The Registration Client shall automatically select and prompt the user with the required action. The challenge sequence should be dynamic and unpredictable. Potential active challenges may include: ● Blink. ● Smile. ● Turn head left/right. ● Look in a speciﬁed direction. ● Other supported facial movements based on the selected liveness technology. The exact challenge set shall depend on the capabilities of the selected liveness/PAD solution. The solution shall include Presentation Attack Detection (PAD) to protect against applicable attacks such as: ● Printed photographs. ● Digital images displayed on screens. ● Video replay attacks. ● Other supported presentation attack techniques. The Registration Client shall not accept a face capture or complete biometric authentication when the liveness/PAD assessment fails. Resident Registration During resident face capture, the system shall verify liveness before accepting the face biometric. The workﬂow shall be: 1. Start face capture. 2. Receive face frames from the L0/L1 device. 3. Perform passive liveness and PAD assessment. 4. Evaluate the liveness quality/conﬁdence. 5. If the conﬁgured threshold is satisﬁed, accept the face capture.

### PAGE 3 ---

6. If the threshold is not satisﬁed, automatically initiate an active challenge. 7. Prompt the resident to perform the dynamically selected action. 8. Analyse the subsequent frames. 9. Accept the face only after successful liveness/PAD veriﬁcation. 10. Allow the resident to retry when veriﬁcation fails, subject to the conﬁgured retry policy. Operator Authentication Liveness and PAD shall be performed as part of biometric authentication for operators. The system shall: ● Receive the operator's face frame stream. ● Perform passive liveness and PAD assessment. ● Initiate active liveness veriﬁcation when required. ● Prompt the operator to perform the required facial action. ● Validate the action using the incoming frame stream. ● Complete authentication only when the conﬁgured liveness/PAD criteria are satisﬁed. Where face veriﬁcation is used for operator authentication, liveness shall provide an additional control to ensure that the matched face is presented by a live person. Supervisor Authentication The same liveness and PAD mechanism shall be applied during supervisor biometric authentication. The system shall ensure that the supervisor is physically present and that the biometric presented during authentication is not a presentation attack. The system shall automatically initiate an active challenge when passive liveness conﬁdence is insufﬁcient. Conﬁguration The face liveness feature shall be conﬁgurable at the Registration Client level. The conﬁguration shall support, at minimum: ● Enable/disable liveness veriﬁcation. ● Conﬁgure passive liveness quality/conﬁdence threshold.

The conﬁguration shall be applicable to both online and ofﬂine operation. Online and Ofﬂine Support The liveness capability shall work in both online and ofﬂine registration environments. Core liveness, PAD, active challenge generation, and facial action validation shall be available locally so that network availability does not determine whether liveness security is enforced. . The system shall maintain consistent liveness behaviour in: ● Online registration. ● Ofﬂine registration. ● Operator authentication. ● Supervisor authentication. Any conﬁguration or model updates that require connectivity shall be securely synchronized when the Registration Client is online. 10. UI/UX Requirements 1. Face Capture Screen The face capture screen shall provide: ● Live face preview. ● Face positioning guidance. ● Liveness status. ● Capture progress. ● Clear indication that liveness is being veriﬁed. The UI should provide a simple experience and avoid exposing technical liveness model information unless required for diagnostics. 2. Passive Liveness While passive liveness is being evaluated, the UI may display: Checking face liveness… The system shall automatically proceed when the liveness criteria are satisﬁed. The user should not be required to perform any action during passive veriﬁcation. 3. Active Challenge When passive liveness does not meet the conﬁgured threshold, the Registration Client shall automatically display a challenge. Examples: Please blink Please smile Please turn your head to the left The prompt shall be generated by the system and shall not require the user to select an action.

### PAGE 9 ---

4. Challenge Feedback The UI should provide real-time guidance while the action is being detected. Examples: Please continue Action detected Hold still Good, face captured successfully 5. Liveness Failure When liveness cannot be established, the UI shall provide clear and actionable guidance. Example: We could not verify face liveness. Please try again. Additional guidance may include: ● Look directly at the camera. ● Ensure your face is clearly visible. ● Improve lighting. ● Keep your face within the capture area. ● Follow the prompted action. 6. PAD Failure When a potential presentation attack is detected, the UI should avoid exposing detailed security information. A generic message may be displayed: Face veriﬁcation could not be completed. Please try again . Detailed PAD information should be available only through appropriate diagnostic or audit mechanisms.
